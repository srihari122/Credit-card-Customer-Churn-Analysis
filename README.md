# Credit-card-Customer-Churn-Analysis
customer churn analysis using Power BI, focusing on customer retention, churn trends, demographics and geographical segmentation.
Business Problem Statement:

The bank has about 10,000 customers, and roughly 1 in 5 (20.37%) has exited. Losing customers is costly because acquiring a new customer is much more expensive than retaining an existing one.

The business currently cannot see:

Which customer segments (gender, card ownership, activity status, geography) churn the most
Whether churn is rising, falling, or seasonal over time
Where to focus retention budget for the greatest impact

Solution:

The dashboard has two pages.

Page 1: Churn Overview

KPI cards give an instant health check of the customer base.
Total Customers by Year & Activity shows growth from 2016 to 2019, with active members consistently slightly above inactive ones.
Exit Customers vs Previous Month is a time-series comparison that reveals spikes and seasonality in exits.
Exit by Gender: Female 55.92%, Male 44.08%.
Exit by Card Category: Credit card holders 69.91%, non-card holders 30.09%.
Slicers (Year, Month, Geography, Activity, Exit, Gender) allow filtering across all visuals.

Page 2: Churn % and Geography

Churn % matrix (Year × Month) uses conditional-format icons (green = low, amber = moderate, red = high churn) to spot problem months quickly.
Customers by Geography: France 50.14%, Germany 25.09%, Spain 24.77%.


Recommendations to the Business:
Target inactive members first. Run re-engagement campaigns (cashback, reward points, personalized offers) to convert inactive customers into active ones.
Launch a retention program for female customers, using surveys and focus groups to understand their pain points and tailor products or offers.
Review the credit card proposition with better rewards, fee waivers, and lower interest tiers, since card holders dominate exits.
Plan seasonal retention campaigns ahead of historically high-churn months (e.g., Jan, May, Jun, Oct).
Prioritize geography-specific strategy: protect France as the core market and investigate whether Germany and Spain have different churn drivers.
